About The Project

The Sales Analytical Dashboard is a data analytics project developed to analyze sales information and generate meaningful business insights. It helps understand total sales, profit, product performance, categories and regional sales using data visualization.

Project Features

Total Sales

Analyze overall sales performance.

Total Profit

Understand business profitability.

Category Analysis

Compare sales across different categories.

Product Analysis

Identify product-wise sales performance.

Regional Analysis

Analyze sales across different regions.

Data Visualization

Present sales insights through Power BI charts.
